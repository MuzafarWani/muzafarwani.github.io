I am a PhD researcher in **High Performance Computing (HPC)** at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur. My research focuses on developing scalable and resource-aware approaches for scientific computing, with an emphasis on **in-situ data analysis and visualization, distributed systems, and resource-aware computing**.

My work explores how large-scale scientific simulations can be processed and analyzed efficiently without excessive data movement. I am particularly interested in making HPC workflows more **adaptive, scalable, and resource efficient** by considering the importance of simulation data, computational resources, and system constraints during execution.

My research includes **importance-driven scientific data analysis and visualization, event-aware scientific computing, and resource-aware distributed learning**. Through this work, I have developed approaches including **LibSimVis, XEventNet, and RHiDE**, and have also worked on resource-aware optimization for distributed and edge-based learning systems.

### Research Interests

- High Performance Computing
- Scientific Computing
- In-Situ Data Analysis and Visualization
- Distributed Systems
- Resource-Aware Computing
- Scientific Data Management
- HPC Applications and Performance Optimization

My broader research goal is to develop **scalable and adaptive computing systems that can efficiently process the growing volume of data generated by modern scientific applications**.
